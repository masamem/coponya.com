The existing coupon system saves codes, terms, expiry and review date. Its store destination is currently shared across coupons for a store, and outbound coupon links are restricted to s.noon.com. Before importing additional programs, add the actual approved tracking domains and decide whether offers need separate destinations. Never substitute generic merchant links and present them as commission-tracked.

After approval, publish five accurate merchant pages and guides based on actual offers. Prepare social posts with the approved code, eligibility and expiry, using the network's permitted channels. Account registration, identity/payment information, contract acceptance and advertiser approval remain necessary before offers can be represented as approved.
